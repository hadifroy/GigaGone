How to free up space on your C: drive

1. Extract the zip and open GigaGone.exe. 2. Pick your drive and click Scan. 3. Look at the biggest folders and files. 4. Tick files you no longer need and send them to the Recycle Bin.

Questions

Why is my C: drive full?

Common causes are large downloads, games, videos, old backups and temporary files. GigaGone scans the drive and shows which folders take the most space, so you can see the cause instead of guessing.

How do I find large files on Windows?

Scan the drive in GigaGone and open the Largest Files tab. Files over 10 MB are listed biggest first, and you can search by name or file type.

Is GigaGone safe to use?

Scanning only reads your drive. Files you delete from the Largest Files list go to the Recycle Bin, so you can restore them. Windows and Program Files are protected from deletion. The Temp Cleaner deletes temporary files permanently, and only after you confirm.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

GigaGone is a new app without a paid publisher certificate, so Windows SmartScreen shows this screen for new files. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Why do some antivirus tools flag it?

New unsigned apps are sometimes flagged by generic heuristics. On our last VirusTotal check, 1 of 64 engines (ClamAV, a generic detection) flagged the zip and 63 did not. You can scan the zip yourself at virustotal.com before running it.

Why does GigaGone ask for administrator permission?

So it can read folders that Windows normally hides, and show their real sizes.
